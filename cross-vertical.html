Cross-Vertical Offers

Six plays that work in any industry.

Use these when the lead is outside our named verticals, when the buyer wants proof before a build, or when the engagement spans years. Strategy from $25K. Bespoke builds from $50–$150K. Embedded operator at $10K/wk. Working software in the first week of every implementation engagement.

The cross-vertical ladder

Wedge → Prove → Foundation → Flagship. Same shape, different scale, all reusable.

Strategy · Wedge CEO · CPO · Chief Strategy $25K · 2 days

The AI First-Principles Workshop

Two-day exec working session. Walk out with 3–5 committed bets, not slides. Often the lead-in for a larger build.

▾
Pain: The exec team has heard a lot about AI but hasn't actually used it well. Decisions are getting made on hype and vendor pitches instead of applied judgment. Every "AI strategy" session devolves into the same circular conversation.

A two-day on-site workshop, half taught and half worked. We bring the applied judgment that comes from shipping production AI portfolios; the team brings their actual problems. Output: a shared mental model of where AI fits, where it doesn't, and 3–5 prioritized bets the team commits to walking out of the room.

Trigger, you'll hear:

"We need to be AI-native." "Board mandate on AI." "Exec team isn't aligned." "Annual offsite is coming up." "New CEO/CPO needs to set direction."

Proof

Run across product, exec, and ops teams; built on the same pattern library that produced the Azul, Alpha, and Pimsleur portfolios.

Champion enablement

Two-page exec readout summarizing the prioritized bets, drops cleanly into a board update or strategy doc.

Engagement

$25K. 2 days on-site. Often co-bought with the board chair before an offsite.

Objection handlers

We've done AI workshops before.

Most are vendor pitches in a workshop wrapper. Ours is half teaching and half working on your real problems. The deliverable is committed bets, not slides.

Why pay for this when OpenAI / Anthropic / Microsoft will run one for free?

Theirs sells you their tool. Ours teaches applied judgment across the tool stack, what to build, what to buy, what to skip. Different incentive, different outcome.

Our exec team is too senior for a workshop.

It's not a workshop in the training sense, it's a working session run by people who've shipped this work. Most senior teams leave saying it was the most useful two days they've spent on AI.

Strategy · Wedge CTO · VP Engineering $25K · 2 days

The AI-Native Engineering Workshop

Hands-on team velocity workshop, agentic workflows, eval discipline, the practices that turn individual gains into team gains.

▾
Pain: Engineering is using AI tools individually but inconsistently. Productivity gains aren't compounding into team velocity. A few engineers are 10x; most are not. The board is asking why output-per-head hasn't moved.

A two-day hands-on workshop for engineers, Cursor, Claude Code, agentic workflows, prompt engineering, eval discipline, AI-native testing. Output: shared toolchain, team-level practices, and a 90-day plan to lock in the velocity gains.

Trigger, you'll hear:

"Our engineers use Cursor, but inconsistently." "Output per head hasn't moved." "Recent layoffs, we need more from the team we have." "New CTO." "10x engineer pressure from the board."

Proof

Run inside multiple client engineering orgs; AE's own engineering team ships production AI daily and the workshop is built from our internal practice.

Champion enablement

Velocity dashboard template, what to instrument, what to measure, what to report up. Lets the CTO defend the investment to the CFO at quarter close.

Engagement

$25K. 2 days hands-on. Pays back inside the first sprint after.

Objection handlers

Our engineers already use Cursor.

Most use it like a fancier autocomplete. The workshop is about the agentic patterns most teams haven't internalized yet, multi-agent workflows, eval-first development, and the practices that turn individual gains into team velocity.

We can't take two days off the roadmap.

The 90-day velocity gains pay back the two days within the first sprint after. We've measured it.

Why not internal training?

Internal training reflects what your team already knows. We bring the patterns we've shipped at clients across industries, including ones your team hasn't seen yet.

Strategy · Wedge Head of Design · VP Design · CDO $25K · 2–3 wks

AI Design Enablement

Two designers are 10x. The rest aren't. We close the gap. Per-designer diagnostic, role-specific workflow installation, every designer ships one real AI-assisted artifact by the end.

▾
Pain: Two of your designers are shipping working prototypes at 10x velocity. The rest are still Figma-first. Tool sprawl is real (Cursor, v0, Lovable, Figma AI, Claude). Managers know it's happening but can't see where the team is stuck or what to install. AI was supposed to fix design bandwidth; instead it's amplified the gap between the early adopters and everyone else.

A 2–3 week diagnostic-plus-installation engagement. We map every designer's current tools, skill levels, blockers, and workflows. We prescribe the stack, install the rituals, and run a two-week sprint where every designer ships one real AI-assisted workflow artifact. Output: the design team thinks AI-first and product-engineering-first, with a manager scoreboard to keep velocity compounding after we leave.

Trigger, you'll hear:

"A couple of designers are 10x with AI; the rest aren't." "We have tool chaos." "I want my designers thinking like product engineers." "Design is the bottleneck on shipping." "New design lead." "CEO is asking why design output hasn't moved."

Proof

Built on AE's own design practice (we ship product daily) and the cross-portfolio pattern library from teams that have already gone through the gap. Not theory; the workflows we've installed in our own studio.

Champion enablement

Manager scoreboard, role-specific rituals, and a sprint artifact each designer can show in their next 1:1. The Head of Design defends the investment with measured velocity, not vibes.

Engagement

$25K. 2–3 weeks. Stack prescription delivered week 1. Sprint artifacts shipped by end of engagement.

Objection handlers

My designers can figure this out themselves.

They are. That's the problem. Two of them have. Three haven't. Six months in, the gap widens. We compress that to two weeks for the whole team.

We've tried tool training before.

Tool training teaches features. We install workflows, rituals, prompts, review gates, role-specific. The deliverable is a designer shipping a real artifact, not a certificate.

Why not just send the team to a course?

Courses are individual. The gap you have is structural, different designers, different blockers, different tools. We diagnose the team and prescribe per-role.

Implementation · Prove CTO · CAIO · P&L owner $50K · 5 days · working software wk 1

AI Bootcamp (Forward-Deployed)

5 days inside the client. Walk in Monday, working software in production by end of week. Most large builds start here.

▾
Pain: The team needs proof that AI can ship in their environment, not a slide, not a pilot that takes a quarter, an actual working thing inside their walls in a week. Procurement won't approve a $1M build until they've seen something real.

Five days of forward-deployed AE PMs and AI engineers inside the client. We pick 1–2 named pains together, work alongside the team for the week, and end with production-ready prototypes plus the buildable plan to scale them. The wedge that consistently converts to a larger build.

Trigger, you'll hear:

"Show me something real." "We need to scope before we commit." "New AI mandate." "Procurement wants proof." "We're between strategy and build."

Proof

This delivery model produced the Azul and Alpha portfolios over time. Most of our largest builds started as a 5-day bootcamp.

Champion enablement

End-of-week prototype demo + buildable plan that the champion walks into procurement with, the prototype is the proof and the plan is the SOW.

Engagement

$50K. 5 days, on-site optional. Often co-bought with the CFO. Most converts to a $50–$150K bespoke build the following week.

Objection handlers

What if your team can't get up to speed on our domain in 5 days?

We've done this in airlines, edtech, and SaaS. The first day is a working diagnostic; we don't pretend to know your business, we ask the right questions and your team teaches us. By day three we're shipping.

Our security review takes 6 weeks.

The bootcamp can run on synthetic data, on a sandboxed environment, or against a non-production system. We don't need full prod access to prove the model.

Why not just hire an AI engineer?

A new hire takes 9–12 months to find and 6 to ramp at ~$300K/yr loaded. The bootcamp ships working software in 5 days for $50K. Hire after, we'll often help you hire, but use the bootcamp to compress the proof.

Implementation · Foundation CIO · CTO · CDO $50–$150K Phase 1 · then $50–$150K per tool

AI Context Foundation

Build the structured-knowledge substrate every internal AI tool needs, then ship the tools on top of it. Phase 1 substrate live in week 1.

▾
Pain: Internal AI tools keep failing adoption. Every new tool gets built on the same broken foundation, scattered docs, unstructured decisions, no institutional memory the AI can retrieve. Investment compounds into nothing.

A two-phase engagement. Phase 1 builds the foundation: structured knowledge, decisions, specs, playbooks, institutional memory, the substrate every internal AI tool actually needs. Phase 2 ships the highest-leverage internal AI tools on top of it, two to three weeks per tool, with adoption built into the design.

Trigger, you'll hear:

"We rolled out an internal AI tool and no one uses it." "Every team is building their own ChatGPT wrapper." "Knowledge management is broken." "We just acquired a company; institutional memory is fragmented."

Proof

Built internally at AE, the Taboada knowledge hub, and applied in client engagements as the substrate underneath every successful internal AI tool.

Champion enablement

Tool roadmap framework, Phase 1 deliverables map directly to a Phase 2 backlog the CIO can defend in next year's budget cycle.

Engagement

Phase 1 substrate: $50–$150K, 4–6 weeks, working ingest in week 1. Phase 2 tools: $50–$150K each, 2–3 weeks per tool.

Objection handlers

We have Notion / Confluence / SharePoint.

Those are storage. The foundation is structure, the decisions, specs, and playbooks reformatted so an AI can actually retrieve them. We sit on top of your storage, not next to it.

Why not just use ChatGPT Enterprise?

ChatGPT Enterprise is the interface. Without a structured knowledge substrate behind it, every answer is half a hallucination. The foundation is what makes the interface useful.

We tried this with [internal AI tool] and it failed.

It failed because the foundation wasn't built first. Most internal AI tools fail this way. We solve that root cause before shipping the tool, that's why ours don't fail the same way.

Implementation · Flagship CEO $10K/wk Embedded Operator + bespoke builds at $50–$150K

The AI Reinvention Partnership

Long-term retainer: fractional Chief AI partner ($10K/wk Embedded Operator) plus dedicated build squad shipping bespoke AI projects at $50–$150K each. The cross-portfolio pattern library, on tap.

▾
Pain: The CEO has decided AI is the company's central bet. They need a partner with senior AI strategy chops, in-house build capability, and cross-industry pattern recognition. Not a vendor, a counterpart who shows up to the executive committee, runs the program, and ships.

A multi-year retainer that combines a fractional Chief AI partner, a dedicated build squad, and access to our cross-portfolio pattern library. Effectively an AI co-CEO function with an embedded shipping team. Designed for the companies whose narrative for the next decade is AI.

Trigger, you'll hear:

"AI is our central narrative." "I need an AI counterpart at the table." "We're betting the company on this." "The board wants to see a real program."

Proof

Multi-year engagements at lighthouse clients producing portfolio-level AI footprints, Azul, Alpha, Pimsleur, Subject.com.

Champion enablement

Quarterly program review template, the format we use to walk the board through the AI portfolio, with EBITDA contribution by program.

Engagement

Embedded Operator at $10K/wk plus bespoke builds at $50–$150K each. Time-and-materials with the option to pause any time, or fixed-scope/fixed-budget per build. Co-bought with the board chair for material commitments. Capacity-limited, two to three running at a time.

Objection handlers

Why not hire a Chief AI Officer?

A CAIO takes 9–12 months to find, lands without the team behind them, and hits velocity 18 months in. We come in week one with the team and the patterns. The CAIO hire works alongside us, we'll help you find the right one, and the partnership graduates as their team scales.

How does this not become consulting bloat?

Time-and-materials with the option to pause any time, or fixed-scope/fixed-budget per build. No annual lock-in. If the metric isn't moving, you stop paying. The retainer grows because the work works, not because the relationship does.

How do we exit?

Built into the engagement. Documentation, capability transfer, and an explicit handoff phase. The IP and the practice live in your company; we're the accelerant, not the dependency.
