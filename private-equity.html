Common objections (Private Equity)

The handful you'll hear across most fund-level conversations. Pull from this; tailor to the specific offer in play.

Our portcos are too different to use one instrument.

The instrument adapts to the vertical, airlines, edtech, SaaS, but the heatmap structure stays the same. Standardization gives you the portfolio view; adaptation gives you the portco answer.

McKinsey or Bain can do this.

They can, for $500K+ per portco, a 15-person pyramid, and a 200-page deck. Ours: $25K per portco for a 12-page heatmap with a buildable plan, delivered by 1–3 senior operators with AI-native product engineering. Run the math across 30 portcos and the answer writes itself.

Our portco CEOs will resist outside diagnostics.

Frame it as a value-creation accelerator the operating partner sponsors, with the CEO as the customer. CEOs leave with a buildable plan their teams can execute.

The portco has its own engineering team.

They're heads-down on this quarter's roadmap. We staff 1–3 senior operators alongside them, transfer ownership, and the portco team scales the second build solo. We're an accelerant, not a permanent fixture.

We use Accenture / Deloitte / a Big 4 for portco implementations.

Their economic model is staffed pyramids; ours is 1–3 senior operators per build, AI-native product engineering throughout. Work gets done in a third of the time at a fraction of the cost. Compare two SOWs side-by-side; the math is uncomfortable for them.
