How to qualify

  • Do they have rich operational + booking data already (not just a thesis)?
  • Is pricing or operations stuck in reactive mode (decisions made after the cost has been incurred)?
  • Is there a measurable P&L line a leader is being judged on — revenue per ASM, OTP, cancellations, fuel?
  • Do they have an internal data team to embed with, or do they need us to lead the build?
  • Data residency, regulatory, or safety constraints? (We've shipped through aviation, government, and Fortune 10 — we work inside them.)
