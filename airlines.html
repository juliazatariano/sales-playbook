Common objections (Airlines)

The handful you'll hear across most carrier conversations. Pull from this; tailor to the specific offer in play.

We already have an RMS, Marketing Cloud, an agency. Why do we need this?

Existing systems optimize within historical patterns. They don't model cross-segment elasticity in real time, propensity at the customer level, or creative throughput. Azul kept PROS and Salesforce; we layered on top and recovered revenue they weren't built to find. Amplification, not replacement.

OEMs already provide health monitoring (GE, Pratt, Rolls-Royce).

OEM tools see the engine. We see the engine plus your operations, maintenance history, network plan, and weather. Different decision surface, different signal.

Regulator concerns: ANAC, FAA, EASA.

We don't replace certified maintenance decisions. We flag what to inspect and when, with explainable signals. The maintenance authority you have today still owns the call.

Why not McKinsey QuantumBlack or BCG GAMMA?

They show up with a 15-person pyramid and a 6-month timeline. We tailor the squad to the work, usually 1–3 senior people, AI-native product engineering throughout. Smaller teams ship faster. Their PoC ends with a recommendation; ours ends with a model in production.

We tried this, ChatGPT plugin, Tableau Pulse, Snowflake Cortex.

Generic NL interfaces don't know your data model. They hallucinate joins, miss your KPI definitions, trip over governance. We train on your schemas, your KPI library, your business rules. The hallucination rate is the metric, and we benchmark it.
