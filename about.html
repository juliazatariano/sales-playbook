How we work

The AE Guarantee

"We'll treat your project like it's our own and do whatever it takes, with a founder-level mentality, to make it spectacularly successful." That isn't a tagline, it's how every engagement is staffed and run.

Working software in week 1

Strategy engagements deliver value the first week. Implementation builds put running software in front of users inside seven days. The first sprint is the proof, not a discovery phase.

Senior squads, no pyramids

1–3 senior people per engagement: PM, AI engineers, ML scientist, tailored to the work. No juniors padding a billable. Every meeting has a senior in the room. The team that scopes the work ships the work.

Flexible commercial approach

Two ways to engage. Fixed scope, fixed budget for clearly-defined projects with a known deliverable. Time and materials for ongoing or evolving work, pause, adjust scope, or exit at any time. No annual lock-ins, no minimum commitment past the current cycle.

Cross-portfolio pattern library

We've shipped production AI in airlines, education, PE, finance, healthcare, and SaaS. Patterns transfer across industries; the team that's seen them ships faster and avoids the wrong builds. Capability transfers to your team at handoff, we exit, you scale.
