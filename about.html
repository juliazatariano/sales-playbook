The Research Mission

Alignment is solvable. The problem is almost no one's really tried.

Existing safety approaches — RLHF, prompt engineering, output filtering — aren't enough. Fewer than one in ten alignment researchers believe current methods will solve core problems before AGI. We focus on neglected directions others overlook: reverse-engineering prosociality in language models, reducing LLM deception, AI consciousness, and physics-based alignment constraints. Our research is peer-reviewed and published. Our collaborators include Anthropic, DARPA, Redwood Research, Princeton, and the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy.
